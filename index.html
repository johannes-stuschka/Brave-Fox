Brave Fox

Save the ducks. Outfox the hunters!

How to play

Story

Deep in the green forest lives a little fox who loves its fluffy duck friends. One day the hunters came to catch them all! Now the brave fox throws its duck friends to safety, right at the hunters, and chases them out of the forest.

Goal

Collect ducks and berries, beat the hunters and defeat the big hunter boss at the end.

Controls

← → Walk
Shift Run
Space Jump
D Throw a duck
